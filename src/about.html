Our mission

Al-banna Tutorials exists to make rigorous, university-level technical education freely available to anyone with a browser. Every course is written the way a good lecture is taught: concepts first, then worked examples, then a practical lab you can run yourself.

The portal covers Artificial Intelligence and LLM agents, Deep Learning, DevOps, robotics with ROS2, Python data analysis, Java, Scrum, Quantum Physics and AI-assisted software development. Several courses are available in both English and Arabic to serve learners across the Arab world.

How the courses work

  • Free and open. No account, no paywall. Content is published under the terms on our Terms page.
  • Structured paths. Each course is a numbered sequence of modules with objectives, examples and a hands-on lab. Use the learning roadmaps to choose a path.
  • Track your progress. Mark modules complete as you go; progress is stored privately in your browser. Finish a course to generate a completion certificate.
  • Interactive where it matters. Simulators, quizzes, practice exams and coding exercises (for example the TorchCode PyTorch problem set and the RNN simulator).

Used in university teaching

Several courses here are the online companion to modules taught in the Department of Data Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University of Petra, Jordan, including Deep Learning, Data Analysis with Python, Programming Language 1, DevOps Lab and Robotics with ROS2.
