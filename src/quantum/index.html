About This Course

This is a conceptual introduction to quantum physics for curious beginners — students, developers, and anyone who has heard “superposition” or “entanglement” and wants to actually understand what those words mean. The only prerequisite is basic algebra: we use real equations like E = hf and λ = h/p and plug real numbers into them, but there is no calculus anywhere. Every module follows the same rhythm: see the puzzle that broke classical physics, learn the quantum idea that solved it in plain English, work a numeric example, then test yourself with an exercise and its solution. The course follows the historical order of discovery, because that's the order in which each idea makes sense.
