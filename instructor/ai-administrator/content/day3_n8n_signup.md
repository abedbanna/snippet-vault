## Before you start

- A **laptop** with Chrome, Edge or Firefox. n8n is hard to use on a phone.
- An **e-mail inbox** you can open now, for the verification message.
- A **Google account** with Google Sheets and Gmail. Your sheet and the Google connection in n8n must use the **same Google account**. Two different accounts is the most common problem of the day.

## Six clicks, together

1. Go to **n8n.io/pricing** and click **Start free trial** (n8n Cloud). No credit card is required.
2. Sign up with your e-mail address and a password.
3. Open the **verification e-mail** and click the link. Not there after two minutes? Check spam and the Promotions tab, then raise your hand in Zoom: the co-host will move you to the "Stuck at sign-up" room.
4. **Name your workspace.** It gets its own address, for example `yourname.app.n8n.cloud`. Write it here: ______________________
5. Answer or skip the **two onboarding questions**.
6. When you see **Your instance is ready!**, click **Start automating**. Stop on the page **Let's build your first automation** and wait for the group.

## Three rules for the trial

| | |
|---|---|
| **14 days** | The trial starts today. After 14 days the workspace and its workflows are deleted. |
| **1,000 executions** | Every run counts, including test runs. Plenty for the course, unless a published workflow runs every minute. |
| **Export every day** | At the end of each session: the three-dots menu next to the workflow name, then **Export JSON**. Keep the files. **Import, From file** loads them into any n8n workspace. |

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| No verification e-mail | Spam, Promotions, wait two minutes, then try another address. |
| "This e-mail already used a trial" | Use another e-mail address. One trial per address. |
| "Sign in with Google" does nothing | Your browser blocked the pop-up. Allow pop-ups for app.n8n.cloud and click again. |
| Google Sheets: "403 Forbidden, the caller does not have permission" | n8n is connected to a different Google account from the one that owns your sheet, or you unticked a box on Google's consent screen. Open the credential, **Switch account**, choose the right account and tick every box. |
| Your sheet shows a green **XLSX** badge | It is an uploaded Excel file. In Google Sheets: **File, Save as Google Sheets**, then use the new copy. |
